# 01 - Product Vision, Pedagogical Frameworks & MVP Scope

---

## 1. Why Skillify Genius Exists

Traditional coding education is broken:
- **Passive Consumption**: Kids follow copy-paste video tutorials without understanding fundamentals.
- **Syntax Memorization**: Obsolete in the age of generative AI and LLM code completion.
- **Badge/Certificate Obsession**: Certificates provide zero proof of engineering capability.

**Skillify Genius** builds genuine cognitive and technical capital by transforming students into autonomous software practitioners through four foundational pillars:
1. **Problem Solving**: Decomposing problems, modeling constraints, designing algorithms.
2. **Self-Learning Ability**: Reading docs, testing hypotheses, debugging unassisted.
3. **Engineering Mindset**: Understanding tradeoffs, testing edge cases, performing Root Cause Analysis.
4. **Builder Mindset**: Shipping deployable software that solves real human needs.

## 2. Proprietary Frameworks

### 2.1 Foundation Framework™ (5-Stage Trajectory)
```
Foundation (Logic & Physics)
     ↓
Exploration (Web & Creative Computing)
     ↓
Creation (Full-Stack Cloud Apps & Databases)
     ↓
Engineering (AI Automation & Security Defense)
     ↓
Innovation (Independent Capstone Defense)
```
*Core Mantra*: **"Foundation before Specialization."**

### 2.2 Problem Solver Framework™ (5 Cognitive Steps)
1. **Deconstruct**: Break ambiguous challenge into inputs, outputs, and constraints.
2. **Architect**: Plan component hierarchy and data flow before touching code.
3. **Build & Test**: Implement modular code with assertions and boundary checks.
4. **Leverage AI**: Use Socratic AI prompts to explore alternatives without spoon-feeding.
5. **Defend**: Articulate engineering tradeoffs and document Root Cause Analysis (RCA).

## 3. MVP Scope vs. Out-of-Scope

### In Scope (Delivered & Verified):
- **4-Phase Curriculum Catalog**: Foundation, Creator Lab, AI Automation, Cyber Safety.
- **Diagnostic Pathfinder Quiz**: 5-step funnel calculating Future Readiness Score.
- **1-on-1 Free Assessment Booking**: Timezone picker, child validator, `.ics` calendar confirmation.
- **Socratic Studio & Code Sandbox**: Progressive 4-tier Hint Ladder and console output.
- **Dynamic Skill Graph™ Telemetry**: 6 capability dimensions calculated from solved challenges & RCAs.
- **Parent Trust Portal**: Plain-English metrics, milestone growth timeline, live project testing links.
- **Mentor Studio**: CRM roster, telemetry calibration sliders, project verification queue.
- **Admin Console**: Trial CRM, admissions lead tracking, interactive SVG growth charts.

### Strict Out of Scope (Do Not Build):
- ❌ Complex automated AI tutors that generate full solutions.
- ❌ Social networks, public chat feeds, or gamified token marketplaces.
- ❌ Bloated third-party payment gateways during pilot phase.
- ❌ Multi-tenant district B2B licensing engines (Reserved for Phase 3).
