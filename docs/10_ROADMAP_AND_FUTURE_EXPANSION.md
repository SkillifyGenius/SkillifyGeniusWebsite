# 10 - Strategic Roadmap & Future Expansion

---

## 1. Multi-Phase Growth Roadmap

```
Phase 1: MVP & Direct-to-Parent Pilot (CURRENT)
  • Deliver founder-led 1-on-1 assessment funnels
  • Prove student project verification & Parent Trust Portal
  • Validate Socratic Hint Ladder & RCA journal telemetry
  • Focus: US, UK, Canada, and Western Europe families

Phase 2: Group Cohorts & Micro-Academy Expansion
  • Small group interactive coding cohorts (max 4-6 students)
  • Mentor matching algorithm based on student interest & timezone
  • Peer code review & collaborative capstone projects

Phase 3: B2B School & District Licensing
  • Multi-tenant private school & homeschool co-op portal
  • Standardized computational thinking assessment reports
  • Teacher training & curriculum licensing packages

Phase 4: Global AI Engineering Ecosystem
  • Native mobile companion apps for Parent Trust telemetry
  • Global student hackathons & verified portfolio badge credentials
```

---

## 2. Guiding Expansion Principles

1. **Pedagogical Integrity**: Never degrade into passive video watching. Real project creation remains mandatory.
2. **Quality over Quantity**: Keep student-to-mentor ratios small.
3. **Data Transparency**: Maintain parents' direct access to live working student software.
